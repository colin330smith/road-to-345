TOMORROW — THE DAY (fullness protocol)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Carbs: a normal gaining day, ~350–400 g, front-loaded. Rice, potatoes, oats, bananas. Glycogen + water = full muscle.
• Sodium: NORMAL, slightly up. Do not cut salt — that flattens you. Salt the food.
• Water: 3–4 L through the day, steady. No "water cutting."
• Avoid: big fatty meals, beans, cruciferous veg, dairy if it bloats you, carbonation, alcohol before. Bloat kills the waist line.
• Meal 2–3 h before the pump: 1½ cups rice + lean protein (chicken/ahi). Nothing heavy after that.
