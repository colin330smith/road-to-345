⚡ THE TWO MODES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

GAIN MODE — the default (about 85% of the next 4 years):
3,050 cal · Protein 190 g · Fat 80 g · Carbs ~415 g
Rate: +0.25 lb/week. Slow is the whole point — slow is what makes it lean.
About 13 lb of scale weight per year, roughly 5–6 lb of it lean.

TRIM MODE — only when body fat hits ~18%:
2,500 cal · Protein 200 g · Fat 70 g · Carbs ~260 g
Rate: −1 lb/week (~0.5% of bodyweight; faster than 1% a week costs muscle — Garthe 2011).
SIX WEEKS AT MOST. Ends early when the waist is down 1" from the trim's start, or the 7-day average is down 6 lb. Resume gaining immediately.
Sleep gate: don't START a trim while sleep averages under 7 h. A deficit on short sleep takes the loss from muscle (Nedeltcheva 2010).
Pre-lift stays whey + banana on trim days. Cut carbs elsewhere.
NEVER during Cycles 5–6.

ADJUSTMENT RULES (check Sundays) — all on 7-DAY AVERAGES, measured from where this phase started:
• Gaining >0.5 lb/wk since the phase began → −150 cal (that's fat, not muscle)
• Flat (<0.1 lb/wk) → +150 cal
• Waist +1" since the phase began → start a trim (if sleep averages 7 h+)
• Waist flat while bodyweight rises → perfect, keep going
The app's Trends tab runs this rule for you.

💊 SUPPLEMENTS — BY EVIDENCE, NOT BY LABEL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚠️ EVERY PRODUCT: NSF Certified for Sport or Informed Sport. No exceptions — whey and caffeine included. You're entering a USPA drug-tested meet: USPA runs its own banned list, tests urine and grants no exemptions. Contamination studies found undeclared banned substances in 12–58% of supplements (Martínez-Sanz 2017).

TIER 1 — proven, take daily, forever
• Creatine monohydrate 5 g — any time, with anything. You're past loading; just never miss. (ISSN, Kreider 2017)
• Whey protein 1–2 scoops/day — a convenience for reaching 190 g, not magic. Certified brand, 24–25 g per scoop.
• Caffeine 3 mg/kg (~250 mg) 50–60 min before the lift (ISSN 2021). At that dose, nothing within ~13 h of bed (Gardiner 2023: 8.8 h for a coffee, 13.2 h for a pre-workout dose). Sleep is the actual bottleneck on lean gain.

TIER 2 — worth it for YOUR situation
• Omega-3: eat the Salmon twice a week and skip the pills. If you don't, 2 g EPA+DHA a day.
• Electrolytes (sodium ~500 mg + potassium) on the incline-walk day and any Florida shift you sweat through.

TIER 3 — only if needed
• Vitamin D3 — only if a blood test says you're low. No clear strength benefit in athletes who aren't deficient (Han 2024). Get it tested at your next physical.
• Magnesium glycinate 300–400 mg before bed if sleep is under 7.5 hr. It's a sleep aid, not a muscle builder.

SKIP: BCAAs (whey covers them), pre-workout blends (caffeine is the only ingredient that works), fat burners, test boosters, glutamine, ZMA. Spend it on chicken thighs.

🎬 PHASE 2 — THE CK CUT (after Wave 45)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Phase 1 is the Pain & Gain build: 200 @ 15%, 170 lb lean, 3/4/5, 15" arms, by around Wave 45.
Phase 2 is the reveal: the SAME 170 lb of lean mass at ~11% — about 190 lb.
That is the 1992 Calvin Klein Wahlberg carrying roughly 25 more pounds of muscle than the ad. Not a smaller Wahlberg. The CK body scaled up.

It is not a multi-year goal. It is a ten-week trim, once Phase 1 is real.

THE GATE: starts only when 200 @ 15% is real on the tape — waist and weekly average, not one good morning. Cutting before the lean mass is there just makes you a smaller version of Phase 1.

THE PROTOCOL
• 10 weeks. Start at 2,500 (trim mode). If the weekly average stalls two weeks running, 2,300. Never lower.
• Protein 215–230 g every day (Helms 2014: 2.3–3.1 g per kg of lean mass). This is what keeps the 170 while the scale drops.
• Lift heavy. Top sets stay; back-offs cut ~20%. The 3/4/5 numbers are HELD, not chased.
• −1 lb/wk. Ten pounds in ten weeks: 200 → 190.
• Refeed days (maintenance, ~3,000) are allowed when hunger bites. They help you stick to the cut; they don't save muscle (ICECAP, Peos 2021).
• Stop at 10%. That is a FLOOR, not a target. Efron at 5% for Baywatch said it wrecked his sleep and his head — that is a photoshoot, not a life.
• Then three weeks back up to maintenance, +200 a week. Do not rebound-bulk.

THE PREVIEW: the spring-2027 mini-cut to ~183 @ 12% is the same look at a lighter weight. You will have seen it once before you do it for real.
