MAINTAIN from today through the peak (Cycles 5–6: Wave 5 starts Mon Nov 9, the max test is Fri Jan 1). Then on Mon Jan 4 the app makes one call: a six-week TRIM if the tape reads 18%+ or the waist is up 1" since today, otherwise back to a slow GAIN.
Why maintenance, not a gain or a cut: holding energy balance through the peak protects the muscle you have and the waist. The strength comes from the training either way (Murphy & Koehler 2022: a deficit cut lean-mass gains, not strength gains). No trims in Cycles 5–6 is still the rule.
Why it failed before: not the maths, the counting. Restaurant food has no number you can trust (meals measured at ±465 kcal, Urban 2016), and grazing on shift is food nobody logs. A diet-resistant group in a classic study turned out to be eating 47% more than they wrote down; their metabolism was normal (Lichtman 1992). Zero Hillstone food removes the one stream you could never weigh.

⚡ THE THREE MODES

MAINTAIN — now, through the peak:
2,800 kcal to start · Protein 200 g · Fat 80 g · Carbs 320 g · Fibre 39 g
2,800 is a starting number (the midpoint of the old GAIN and TRIM). Your own logs replace it (below).

TRIM — only at ~18%, never in Cycles 5–6:
2,500 kcal (or 500 under your measured maintenance) · Protein 200 g · Fat 72 g · Carbs 263 g
Rate: −1 lb/week (~0.5% of bodyweight). Faster than about 1% a week risks muscle (Garthe 2011; the 0.5–1% band is Helms 2014). Six weeks at most. Ends early when the waist is down 1" or the 7-day average is down 6 lb.
Sleep gate: don't START a trim until sleep is logged 3+ nights this week, averages 7 h+, and no night was under 6 h. A deficit on short sleep takes the loss from muscle (Nedeltcheva 2010).
The pre-lift shake keeps its banana on a trim. The deficit comes from smaller starch and fat portions, never protein.

GAIN — after the trim, or on Jan 4 if no trim is due:
3,050 kcal (or 125 over your measured maintenance) · Protein 190 g · Fat 78 g · Carbs 397 g
Rate: +0.25 lb/week. Slow is what makes it lean.

The macros now add up exactly: protein and fat are set, carbs are the remainder. Every calorie change moves the carbs.

📐 YOUR MAINTENANCE, MEASURED
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Maintenance = what you ate on complete days − the energy in your weight trend (Hall 2008). The app does it on Trends:
• The first 7 days after a switch are left out: water, glycogen and gut content move 4–5 lb with no fat in it.
• A provisional number after 21 counted days (from today: Sun Nov 29), the rolling 28-day number after that (Sun Dec 6), always with a ± band.
• It needs 6 of every 7 days marked complete and 5+ weigh-ins a week. A half-logged day reads as eating less.
• One- and two-week scale changes are mostly water (Bhutani 2017), which is why it waits four weeks.
• It converts the trend at 3,500 kcal per lb and never uses that to forecast (Hall 2011).
• The target moves only when the estimate is past its ± band and 100 kcal. You tap to accept it. Then MAINTAIN, TRIM and GAIN all move with it.
Strict means steady, not perfect: the same weighed food logged the same way every day calibrates itself. An unlogged meal now and then cannot be calibrated out.

🧊 THE SHIFT BAG
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Zero restaurant food means the bag is the kitchen.
• Every night: freeze two gel packs (5×3" or bigger) and a 1 L water bottle.
• Insulated bag + a fridge thermometer. Layer: gel pack, the later meal, the bottle, the earlier meal, gel pack on top. Sauces in their cups.
• Bag closed, in the AC or shade, never the car. Above 90 °F food gets 1 hour out of the cold, not 2.
• At each meal check the thermometer. Above 40 °F or soft packs: the food goes. Backup: an unopened can of tuna, whole fruit, certified whey mixed at the break.
• Reheat to 165 °F. French dip jus at a rolling boil into a thermos preheated with boiling water.
• Never packed: seared ahi (warm tuna can form histamine that cooking does not destroy), anything still warm, anything raw-marinated.

📏 THE RULES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. ZERO RESTAURANT FOOD. No staff meal, no tasting, no grazing on shift. Every bite is your own weighed food.
2. Weigh every component on a gram scale, raw or cooked as the recipe says. No eyeballing.
3. Log before the first bite: one tap on the prepped portion.
4. Oil, butter, sauces and dressings are weighed and counted in full.
5. Mark the day complete when the last bite is logged.
6. Same plan seven days a week, weekends and holidays included (Gorin 2004: maintainers who ate the same way all week held their weight 1.5× more often). Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day are plan days.
7. No liquid calories: water, black coffee, zero-sugar certified Celsius. The whey is weighed and logged.
8. Alcohol: 2 drinks at most, logged (~100 kcal each before the mixer). Never the night before a squat or deadlift day, none on a trim, none in a test week.
9. Protein at every meal: 45–60 g at the three meals, 25–30 g at the shake and the snack.
10. Weigh in every morning: after the bathroom, before food or drink, same scale.
11. The plan changes only on the app's call, never on one morning (Turicchi 2020: weight swings about 0.35% within a normal week).
12. Batch food by cook date + 3; reheat to 165 °F; the bag stays at 40 °F or below.

💊 SUPPLEMENTS — CERTIFIED, FROM COSTCO WHERE POSSIBLE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚠️ EVERY PRODUCT: NSF Certified for Sport or Informed Sport. No exceptions — whey and caffeine included. USPA drug-tested runs its own banned list, tests urine and grants no exemptions. Contamination studies found undeclared banned substances in 12–58% of supplements (Martínez-Sanz 2017). Checked against both lists on 2026-11-01: no Kirkland Signature product is on either.

TIER 1 — daily, forever
• Creatine monohydrate 5 g (Kreider 2017). Costco: Sports Research Creatine Monohydrate capsules (Informed Sport; 4 capsules = 5 g).
• Whey: the 33 g pre-lift shake, weighed. Costco: Ascent 100% Whey (Informed Sport). Not Kirkland whey; not ON Gold Standard in Costco's sizes and flavours.
• Caffeine 3 mg/kg (~250 mg) 50–60 min before the lift (Guest 2021); nothing within ~13 h of bed at that dose (Gardiner 2023). Black coffee, or Celsius (NSF Certified for Sport).

TIER 2 — your situation
• Omega-3: fatty fish twice a week. The rotation has sockeye every other Sunday; swap the salmon plate in on an off night to make it twice. Otherwise 2 g EPA+DHA from a certified fish oil.
• Electrolytes on sweaty shifts: DripDrop Zero Sugar, Lemon Lime and Watermelon sticks only (NSF lists those two).

TIER 3 — only if needed
• Vitamin D3 only if a blood test says you're low (Han 2024). Magnesium glycinate 300–400 mg before bed if sleep is under 7.5 h.

Before the first scoop of any new tub: the mark on the package, then the product by name, flavour and size in the NSF app or on sport.wetestyoutrust.com, and the lot on the tub. No match, no use.

🎬 PHASE 2 — THE CK CUT (after Wave 45)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Phase 1 is the Pain & Gain build: 200 @ 15%, 170 lb lean, 3/4/5, 16" arms, by around Wave 45.
Phase 2 is the reveal: the SAME 170 lb of lean mass at ~11% — about 190 lb. Calvin Klein-era Wahlberg (the 1992 ad) carrying roughly 25 more pounds of muscle.
It is not a multi-year goal. It is a ten-week trim, once Phase 1 is real.
THE GATE: starts only when 200 @ 15% is real on the tape — waist and weekly average, not one good morning.
• 10 weeks, −1 lb/wk, 2,300 kcal on the same home-cooked plan.
• Protein 215–230 g every day (Helms 2014: 2.3–3.1 g per kg of lean mass).
• Lift heavy. Top sets stay; back-offs cut ~20%.
• Refeed days are allowed when hunger bites. They help you stick to the cut; they don't save muscle (Peos 2021).
• Stop at 10%. That is a floor, not a target. Then three weeks back up to maintenance.
THE SHREDDED BACK is this phase's job. Back work builds the muscle under the fat; it does not burn the fat over it (Ramirez-Campillo 2022). Judge it by a monthly rear relaxed and rear lat-spread photo next to the waist.
