# Efficiency audit — stimulus per minute, fatigue, and the yearly structure

Status: DRAFT 1 (written early so nothing is lost; numbers below are computed from `dossier.json` with node, sources opened on PubMed on 2026-11-19). Being extended.

## Verdict (draft)

The week is well built; the year is where the minutes leak. A full week is 147-150 working sets, but over the 24-week macro that ends at the June 2027 meet (Waves 7-12) the plan averages 106 working sets a week: 72% of the headline week. Side delts read 14.5 fractional sets a week on the body map but average 9.8 across the macro; lats 15.5 vs 10.0; mid back 8.5 vs 6.6; calves 6 vs 4.5. The dose-response curve acts on what is done across the year, not on the best week.

## Sources opened so far (PubMed record read)

- Pelland JC, Remmert JF, Robinson ZP, Hinson SR, Zourdos MC. Sports Med 2026;56(2):481-505. doi:10.1007/s40279-025-02344-w. 67 studies, 2,058 participants.
- Coleman M et al. PeerJ 2024;12:e16777. RCT, 39 trained, 9 weeks, one week off at the midpoint: hypertrophy the same, lower-body strength better without the break.
- Pancar Z et al. Sci Rep 2026;16(1):10299. Within-subject, 19 untrained men, 8 weeks, deload in weeks 4 and 8 (1 session, 2 sets): same growth and 10RM.
- Rogerson D et al. Sports Med Open 2024;10(1):26. Survey, 246 competitive strength and physique athletes: deload every 5.6 ± 2.3 weeks, 6.4 ± 1.7 days.
- Winwood PW et al. Sports Med 2026;56(12):3099-3123. Systematic review of self-reported tapers: volume peaks 4-6 weeks out, intensity 2-3 weeks out, taper volume cut ~40-45%, cessation 1.5-4 days.
- Zhang X et al. Sports Med 2025;55(4):953-975 (supersets). Havers T et al. Sports Med Open 2026;12(1):38 (drop sets). Bradshaw JT et al. J Strength Cond Res 2026;40(6):638-649 (myo-reps, trained men). Cowley N et al. Sports Med 2026;56(8):1955-1977 (advanced methods network meta-analysis).
