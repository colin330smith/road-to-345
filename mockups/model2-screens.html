Exercise scientist

Dose you can see

  • Gold is your goal muscles, blue is support. Brightness is weekly sets, compounds counted at half (Pelland 2025). The gold lands on delts, upper chest, arms and upper back, which is the point.

Personal trainer

One cue, one reason, one ladder

  • The rep ladder on every accessory: 2 sets hitting the target moves you up a step next time (10 → 12 → 15), then the weight goes up. You see the next step before you lift.
  • One hard set per muscle: only the anchor's last set goes to RPE 9–10 plus partials, and it's flagged on the set dots.
  • A readiness check that changes the plan: Yellow takes 5% off the back-offs and caps accessories at 2 sets. Red is 155 × 3 × 3, done in 30–45 minutes.
