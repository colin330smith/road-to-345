# Audit 2027 — Legs, hips, calves, abs and the base for the squat and deadlift

Status: DRAFT IN PROGRESS (skeleton written first; sources are being opened and checked one by one — anything not yet opened is marked "to verify").

## Verdict (draft)

The lower-body plan is structurally sound: two squat days, one deadlift day, knee-flexion plus hip-extension hamstring work at long length, straight-knee calf work, leg press held at RPE 8. The open questions are (1) whether the squat, the lagging lift, gets enough quad work and a third quad exposure, (2) the things only he can answer (bar position, depth, where the squat fails), and (3) small zero-minute upgrades (calf partials on every set, glute credit in the body map).

- Seated leg curl + RDL covers knee flexion and hip extension at long length (Maeo 2021; Kubo 2019 — to verify).
- No hip thrust: squats grew the glutes as much (Plotkin 2023 — to verify).

- Straight-knee calf work with a paused stretch and lengthened partials (Kinoshita 2023; Kassiano 2023 — to verify).
