# Recovery, sleep, cardio and health — 2027 audit findings (DRAFT, in progress)

Status: skeleton written 2026-11-19; sources are being opened and checked one by one. Anything not yet opened is marked (to verify) and will move to "Could not verify" if it cannot be opened.

## Verdict (draft)

The recovery scaffolding is better than most programs: submaximal main lifts, a deload every fourth week, a real taper, one full rest day, a caffeine rule that protects sleep, and a sleep gate on dieting. The weak point is the one the brief names: sleep opportunity. Six 7:00 AM sessions after evening restaurant shifts caps sleep on closing nights at about 5 to 5.5 hours, and the app only uses the sleep log to gate a trim. Aerobic work is one 20-minute incline walk a week.

3. TEST-NYE — the max test is 7 AM on Fri Jan 1 2027, the morning after New Year's Eve service. Verify with him.
4. TRIM-GATE — the trim's sleep gate (7-day average 7 h+, no night under 6 h) may never open for a closing-shift worker. (to verify: Nedeltcheva 2010, Wang 2018)
5. CARDIO — one 20-minute incline walk a week; cheapest effective dose. (to verify: WHO 2020, Ding 2025, Paluch 2022, Schumann 2022, Lundberg 2022)
6. COOLDOWN — 5 min of static stretching on the clock five days a week (25 min) with no recovery or injury benefit. (to verify: Herbert 2011, Afonso 2021, Alizadeh 2023)
7. PAIN-RULE — a graded pain rule and a per-exercise swap instead of a whole Yellow day. (to verify: Smith 2017, Silbernagel 2007)
8. HEALTH — blood pressure and a baseline lipid panel are not in the plan. (to verify: USPSTF 2021, AHA/ACC 2025, Grundy 2018)
9. ALCOHOL — the cap is fine; add the sleep reason and the post-close rule. (to verify: Gardiner 2025)
